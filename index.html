What we do

Every job gets a ticket. Every ticket gets finished.

JOB #01 GENERAL

Full Servicing

Oil change, filter, chain-sprocket, brakes, clutch and a full running check — the works, done right.

JOB #02 REPAIR

Engine & Electrical Repair

Engine rebuilds, starting issues, wiring faults, battery and self-start problems — diagnosed properly, not guessed.

JOB #03 CUSTOM

Bike Modification

Custom exhausts, handlebars, seats, paint & wraps, LED setups and full styling builds to your spec.

JOB #04 BODY

Denting & Painting

Panel beating, dent removal and factory-match or custom paint jobs finished to a showroom shine.

JOB #05 WHEELS

Tyres & Wheel Alignment

Tyre replacement, wheel balancing, alignment and suspension tuning for a smoother, safer ride.

JOB #06 PICKUP

Pickup & Drop

Can't make it to the shop? We pick up your bike, service it, and drop it back — no downtime for you.
